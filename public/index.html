CodeTeach

This tutor won't give you the answer.

We could. ChatGPT and Copilot happily will. That feels good for five minutes and teaches you nothing.

CodeTeach works differently. It'll ask questions instead of giving answers. It'll make you write a one-sentence hypothesis before every hint. It'll be slower, and slightly annoying on purpose.

Here's why: the students who sit with a hard bug and articulate what they think is wrong are the ones who learn to debug. The ones who paste the fix aren't.

You'll still have to solve it. That's the deal.

Privacy notice

      
Last updated: 2026-10-07

    


    

      
What we collect

      

        CodeTeach Debug Tutor records your work while you use it. This includes
        your enrollment details (name, email, the classes you've joined), the
        debug sessions you run, the hypotheses you write while debugging, the
        post-mortems you complete afterward, and telemetry about when hints are
        served. Your tutor may also add notes and feedback to your work.
      

      

        As you work, we also take snapshots of your code editor and
        error output at key moments — when you ask for a hint, click
        "I fixed it," or leave the editor. These snapshots, along with the
        events listed above, let your tutor replay your session to
        understand how you approached the bug. We do not
        record individual keystrokes, and nothing outside the code editor is
        captured. You can see everything we've recorded about your sessions
        from the My sessions tab.
      


      
Why we collect it

      

        The data powers the tutor dashboard that helps your instructor spot
        where you're stuck, and it powers the reflection prompts you see after
        each exercise. Nothing is used to train third-party models, and nothing
        is sold or shared with advertisers.
      


      
Who can see it

      

        Only the instructor of each class you join can see your data, and only
        for exercises assigned to that class. Data from one class is not
        visible to instructors of another.
      


      
How long we keep it

      

        Your data is kept until it is deleted — either by you (via Account
        settings) or by your instructor (via the student or class data
        deletion controls). Deletions are permanent and cannot be undone.
      


      
Your rights

      

        You can export a copy of your data at any time from your student
        dashboard. You can request that all of your data be deleted by using
        the Delete my account option in Account settings, or
        by asking your instructor. When deleted, your debug history,
        hypotheses, post-mortems, telemetry, tutor notes, and feedback are
        removed from the system.
      


      
Data location

      

        Data is stored on the server hosting this application. It is not
        transferred to third parties.
      


      
Contact

      

        For questions about your data, contact the instructor of your class,
        or reach out to the application maintainer.
